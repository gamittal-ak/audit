How certificate findings are interpreted

For observed leaf certificates with a lifetime of 31 days or less: more than 10 days remaining prompts a renewal review; 8–10 days is a warning; 7 days or less is critical. Expired and not-yet-valid certificates are critical. Configured CA certificates and pinned-certificate contexts keep their expiry warnings.

Certificate lifetime or issuer does not confirm automated renewal. Confirm ownership, renewal status, and replacement deployment with the origin owner.

Findings use the certificate observed at the audit's collection time{% if audit_timestamp %} ({{ audit_timestamp | replace('T',' ') | replace('Z',' UTC') }}){% endif %}. Observed leaf means the endpoint presented this certificate; the probe does not validate its trust chain, hostname, Akamai delivery, or renewal automation.
